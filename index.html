Welcome · Unibic Field Toolkit

12 tools, one screen

Everything a Unibic rep needs to price, negotiate, and plan — in 30 seconds.
W1
What
A pocket calculator with 12 field tools: deal margin, break-even discount, scheme stacking, stock cover, return risk, shelf decisions, route ranking, day tally, target gap.
W2
Who
Unibic field reps and area managers. One file, one phone, no login.
W3
When
In the store, at the order, on the route, and at the end-of-day report. Day Tally auto-clears at midnight.
W4
Where
Any phone browser. Works offline. Nothing is sent to a server — your numbers stay on your device.
W5
Why
Reps make margin decisions in their head and get them wrong. This makes every decision a number — with a break-even, a counter-offer, and a shareable verdict.
How — 3 things to know
① Pick a tab. ② Move the sliders. ③ Tap the ? on any input to learn what it means. The verdict updates live. When it matters, share it on WhatsApp.
